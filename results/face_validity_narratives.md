# Face Validity — Sample Narratives (supporting material)

Five narratives each from Methods C and D, randomly sampled (seed=99), with LLM-judge ratings on four 1–5 scales.

> **Self-evaluation bias caveat.** These ratings come from an LLM judge in the same model family (claude-sonnet-4-6) that generated the narratives. A model rating its own family's output is not an independent assessment and likely skews lenient. In a fuller study, human ratings or a different model-family judge would be the right next step. Read these as indicative, not definitive.

Scales: **realistic** (5=most believable), **generic** (5=most interchangeable), **stereotyped** (5=most stereotyped), **caricature** (5=most exaggerated).

**Rating:** realistic=4, generic=3, stereotyped=2, caricature=1

**Judge justification:** Specific details like Fresno, garden center, and Tuesday river walks add believability, though the overall profile touches familiar working-class older-woman tropes.


> I grew up in Fresno and never really left California, though I always meant to. I took some community college classes back in the eighties — nursing prerequisites, mostly — before life got busy with kids and bills. These days I work part-time as a cashier at a garden center, which suits me fine because I love being around plants and chatting with the regulars. My two closest friends are women I've known since our kids were in elementary school together. We walk along the trail by the river on Tuesday mornings and tell each other the truth.

Online, I'm on Facebook mostly — family photos, local buy-nothing groups, the occasional recipe video. I don't trust everything I read and I appreciate when people keep things civil. Faith, hard work, and honesty matter most to me.
